## Full description (Google Play max 4,000 characters; App Store max 4,000)
100X Longevity turns your own health numbers into a clear Health Tier — 100X, 95X, 90X, 80X, 70X or Below 70X — and shows exactly what is holding you back.

Enter what you have: age, weight and waist to start, then add blood tests, fitness tests and recovery data whenever you get them. Your tier updates as you type.

WHAT YOU GET
• Your estimated Health Tier and Health equivalent
• A scorecard across seven areas: fitness, body composition, metabolic health, cardiovascular, inflammation, kidney/liver and sleep/recovery
• Your main bottleneck and the markers driving it
• Your assigned 100x Longevity program and next target tier
• Your recommended starting meal plan (1500–3000 calories)
• A personal matrix showing every benchmark for your age and sex
• Optional Ask tab: get plain-language answers about your results

AGE-ADJUSTED FITNESS
VO2 max, push-ups, pull-ups and wall sit are scored against your age bracket and sex. Clinical markers are scored against fixed benchmark ranges.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your account holds only your email. Your answers stay on your device. The optional Ask feature only sends your question and results after you allow it. Delete your account anytime in the app.

IMPORTANT
100X Longevity is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It provides benchmark-based estimates for general wellness information only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, and never start, stop or change medication based on this app. If you have urgent symptoms, seek medical care immediately.
