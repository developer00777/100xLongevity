100X Longevity — Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]. This policy explains how the 100X Longevity Assessment app ("the app"), published by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we"), handles your information.

What the app does

The app estimates a benchmark-based Health Tier from health and fitness values you type in, such as age, weight, waist, blood test results and fitness tests. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

Your account

You sign in with an email address and a password. On our server (hosted by Cloudflare, Inc.) we keep only your email address, a salted one-way hash of your password (never the password itself), the dates your sign-in sessions start and expire, the times of recent failed sign-in attempts (kept for 15 minutes, to block password guessing), and the times you used the Ask feature (kept for 24 hours, to enforce an hourly limit; no question text). You can delete your account at any time in the app (Rules → Delete my account); this immediately removes your account and all of the records above, and the answers saved for that account on the device.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter in the assessment is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, separately for each account that signs in on that device. We do not receive these values unless you use the optional Ask feature described below. You can delete all of your answers at any time in the app (Rules → Delete all my answers) or by uninstalling the app.

Optional Ask feature (AI answers)

The Ask tab lets you ask questions about your results. Before your first question, the app asks for your permission. If you allow it, each question is sent together with your current assessment results (the values you entered and the resulting tiers) over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it through OpenRouter, Inc. (an AI model gateway) to Anthropic, PBC (the provider of the Claude AI service) to generate an answer. We do not send your name, email, phone number or device identifiers to OpenRouter or Anthropic. Our server checks that you are signed in, but does not store your questions or results after the answer is returned. OpenRouter and Anthropic process this data as our service providers under their commercial terms; see openrouter.ai/privacy and anthropic.com/legal for their policies. You can withdraw permission at any time in the app (Rules → Turn off sharing with Claude).

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show advertising, use your data for tracking, or share it with data brokers. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Security

All data sent to our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2 hashes. On phones your sign-in session is kept in the device's secure storage (Keychain or Keystore). Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security.

Your rights

Because we do not keep your assessment data on our servers, the fastest way to delete it is in the app. You can delete your account and its server records in the app (Rules → Delete my account). For any privacy question or request, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].
